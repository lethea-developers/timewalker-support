Common questions

I can't sign in. Your account is created by your restaurant manager. If you don't have login details, ask your manager, or contact us at the email above.

My check-in/check-out QR code isn't working. Make sure your device has an active internet connection and the code hasn't expired (codes are valid for 30 seconds). Generate a new code and try again.

My hours or payroll look incorrect. Contact your restaurant manager first, since shift records are managed on their end. If the issue persists, email us.
